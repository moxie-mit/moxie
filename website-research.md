  Final Direction for Moxie
After studying these websites, the common lesson is:
> A great club website should not simply display information — it should tell the club's story.
1· Tell the Story
On the homepage, use visuals + animations + content to tell Moxie's story.
2· Establish Identity Immediately
At the top of the homepage, introduce Moxie with a strong statement:
> Moxie is not just a club.
Follow it with a short phrase that communicates what Moxie stands for.
3· Animate With Purpose
> Animations should not be for decoration. They should support the story.
Every major animation should have a reason to exist.
4· Make Moxie's Significance Obvious
A visitor should understand:
What Moxie is → What Moxie does → Why Moxie matters
through storytelling, visuals, interaction, and clear content.
